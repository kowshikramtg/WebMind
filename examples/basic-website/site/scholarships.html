Scholarships and financial aid

Merit scholarship

The merit scholarship covers 50 percent of tuition for students with a secondary school grade average of 90 percent or higher. It is renewed each year if the student keeps an average of 80 percent.

Need-based grants

Need-based grants of up to 2,000 per year are available for students from low-income households. Applicants submit household income documents together with the admission application.

Scholarship eligibility criteria

Scholarship applicants must first meet the general admission eligibility requirements, be enrolled full-time, and apply before April 30.
