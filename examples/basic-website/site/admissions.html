Admissions

How to apply

Applications are submitted online through the admissions portal. You need a completed application form, your school transcripts, one recommendation letter and a short personal statement.

Eligibility requirements

To be eligible for an undergraduate program you must have completed secondary school with a minimum grade average of 70 percent, including mathematics in the final year. Applicants whose first language is not English must show English proficiency at level B2 or higher.

Mature applicants over 23 years old without the standard qualifications may be admitted after a placement assessment.

Application deadlines

The main application deadline is March 31 for programs starting in September. Late applications are reviewed until June 15 if places remain.

Tuition fees

Tuition for undergraduate programs is 4,800 per academic year. Continuing education certificates cost between 600 and 1,200 depending on the course.
