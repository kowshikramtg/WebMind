Our Services

Lumen Valley Institute offers four main services to students, companies and the local community.

Undergraduate programs

We offer three-year bachelor programs in Software Engineering, Electrical Engineering and Data Science. Classes are small, with an average of 24 students per class, and every program includes a semester-long industry project.

Research consulting

Our research department helps local companies with applied research projects, prototyping and data analysis. Consulting engagements typically last between three and nine months.

Continuing education

Evening and weekend courses for working professionals, including certificates in cloud computing, machine learning and project management. Most certificates take twelve weeks.

Career services

Career advisors help students with internships, CV reviews and interview preparation. The annual career fair takes place every October.
